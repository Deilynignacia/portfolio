PROJECT 1 / HERITAGE

Heritage Postage Stamp 🌿

This original heritage postage stamp is a tribute to my great-grandmother, Juana López Blanco, as well as an exploration of how graphic design can be used to preserve personal and family history.

Read Design Reflection ⭸

Using the only photograph of Juana I have as a reference, I created a digital illustration in Sketchbook, a mobile drawing application, reimagining how she might have looked in her younger years.

Throughout the design process, I had the opportunity to explore the principles of visual hierarchy as a way to guide the viewer's attention. For this reason, the portrait occupies most of the composition, establishing my great-grandmother as the focal point. Meanwhile, other elements, such as her name, the stamp's price, and the name of the country, are arranged around her to distribute the visual weight across the design.

I chose a color palette of muted blues, purples, and grayish tones, along with a hand-drawn, textured style, to evoke the appearance of a vintage postage stamp. Contrast, spacing, and alignment helped me organize the layout and reinforce the visual hierarchy. I also used different typefaces, sizes, and orientations to distinguish the price, her name, and the country.

Ultimately, this piece represents more than an exercise in visual composition. It is a personal interpretation of my family history, combining illustration and graphic design techniques to transform an old photograph into a small tribute to someone who shaped such a significant part of my family's story.

PROJECT 2 / STICKER D

Sticker Brand

This sticker is a brand project based on my initial.

Read Design Reflection ⭸

For this personal brand sticker, I chose a design centered around the first letter of my name. I wanted to create something that was both recognizable and representative of my personal identity. I decided to use a strong letter structure with a brick-like texture over a watercolor background. Throughout the design process, I refined the original concept by adding small details and adjusting the composition and textures.

For the color palette, I chose a predominantly monochromatic scheme using different shades of purple. I wanted to create a cohesive design with strong contrast and defined edges. The letter itself establishes a clear visual hierarchy, while the watercolor splashes create layers around the main focal point and help distribute the visual weight across the composition. Ultimately, I tried to reflect structure and creativity, two elements that I consider important to my personal brand.
